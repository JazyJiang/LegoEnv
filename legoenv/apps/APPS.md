# App capability cards

The vocabulary has 212 registered apps; 193 of them have a per-app adapter
module under `apps/adapters/<app>/` that passes the onboard check. Read the
capability card and the register module of every app you select. `login: null`
means the app has no login call. `carriers: {}` means the app cannot host a
policy document; use the other app's declared carrier. Native `login.arguments`
selectors and ids keep their real parameter names and types. Do not invent
missing writes, read tools, external AI execution, transfers, binary file IO or
network delivery.
